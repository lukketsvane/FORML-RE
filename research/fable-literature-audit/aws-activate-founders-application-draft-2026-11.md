## Proposed application copy

### Startup description

FINNE KODER is an independent Norwegian design-technology practice developing digital tools and experimental software products. The work combines interaction design, computational methods, visual systems, and research into how people structure complex information and creative work.

### Product / problem statement

The current product work explores software for visual thinking and knowledge organisation. This includes Cells.garden, a spatial workspace for organising projects, motivations, ideas and outcomes, and FORMLÆRE, a digital research publication and formal framework for design theory. The goal is to develop tools that help individuals and teams explore, structure and communicate complex ideas.

### How AWS will be used

AWS will support development, testing and deployment of the company's software prototypes and research tools. Amazon Bedrock will be used for controlled evaluation of foundation models for literature analysis, software development assistance, and structured synthesis of public research material. Initial model testing will use synthetic prompts and public documents; outputs will be reviewed by a human. The immediate technical pilot is a comparative evaluation of Claude Fable 5.2 for a literature-audit workflow.
